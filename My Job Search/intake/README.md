# intake - drop your documents here

Put anything that describes your work in this folder: old resumes or CVs, cover letters, essays, a thesis,
papers, reports, performance reviews, certificates. Word (.docx), PDF (.pdf), plain text (.txt) and
Markdown (.md) are read; anything else is listed but skipped.

Then tell Claude: **read my documents**. It extracts the text (copies go into `_extracted/`; your originals are
never changed), drafts a first version of your profile from it, and reads every item back to you before any
of it counts. Nothing from this folder becomes an approved fact until you confirm it in the conversation.

This folder stays on your computer. Nothing in it is uploaded, sent, or copied anywhere by jobkit.
