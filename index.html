What makes it different

It starts from you, not from one resume and a keyword list.

Interview first

It learns your real story

It reads your old documents, then asks what you built yourself versus only used, and which numbers you can stand behind. Nothing counts until you confirm it.

Every posting

It reads the jobs for you

Postings straight from employers' own job boards (Greenhouse, Lever, Workday, Oracle, iCIMS and more), judged on what the job actually asks, not its title.

Worth your time

Two judges score the fit

Each posting that might fit is scored on a fixed scale, and you hear which ones to skip and why. A judge has to quote the posting to prove it read it.

Your story

A resume for each job

It leads with the experience that job needs, including that project you would have forgotten. Every line is something you approved; a made-up line is refused.

Your call

You stay in charge

It drafts; you decide and apply on the employer's own page. A simple tracker shows everything you have built and applied to.

Questions

Do I need to know how to code, or use GitHub?

No. Download the zip from this page and talk to it in plain English. Claude runs every technical step.

How long does setup take?

Two to four hours of conversation, in as many sittings as you like (shorter if you drop in your old documents first). Type set me up again any time to continue where you left off.

Does it work for my field?

It is built for any field: nursing, software, finance, research, teaching, government, nonprofits and more. It also finds the job board of any employer you name.

Is my information private?

Everything about you stays in the My Job Search folder on your computer. jobkit only uses what you put there and what you tell it.

What are the limits?
  • The judges are AI, not recruiters: a score is a judgment of fit, not a promise of an interview.
  • It searches the job boards in the packs you choose plus employers you add; some sites block automated reading.
  • It never invents anything, but it cannot verify what you tell it.
  • Tested on Mac so far; Windows is supported but new. The full list is in the README.
Something went wrong. Where do I tell you?

Tell Claude what you see first: it fixes most problems itself. If it cannot, report it here.
