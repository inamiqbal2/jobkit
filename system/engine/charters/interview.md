# CHARTER: INTERVIEW PROTOCOL (any invite, screen, or prep request)

Scope note: jobkit's core is discovery / screening / judgment / resume building. Interview prep is
supported as drafting only; nothing is sent, and the user's live processes are listed in
`my-rules.md` ("LIVE PROCESSES"), never in engine files.

0. Log it: state.db `Rejection/Interview = INTERVIEW <date> - <format> (<name, TITLE> - <screen type>)`,
   Status, Days to Resp. OUTCOMES_LOG row.
1. IDENTIFY THE INTERVIEWER (public sources only). Screen type changes everything: a recruiter screen
   dies on logistics and plain English (give them one sentence they can repeat); a hiring-manager or
   technical round goes deep (never bluff - flagged gaps get honest scripted answers).
2. Two background research tasks in parallel, each writing one file in the application folder:
   (a) `CompanyIntel_<Co>.md` - business, funding, technology, delivered-vs-announced, risks, recent
   news; every claim sourced; "NOT VERIFIED" allowed.
   (b) `RoleGapAnalysis_<Co>.md` - reads the JD + publications.md + skills-inventory first; day-to-day of
   the role, knowledge map, gaps ranked by probe-likelihood x damage with honest answers, bridges from
   real experience, tiered study plan with real sources, ~20 Q&A grounded ONLY in verified experience.
   Research tasks never inflate; they flag uncertain claims for the user.
3. `InterviewPrep_<Co>.md`: scheduling reply DRAFT (approval before anything is sent, by the user, from
   their own mail client), calibration, story cards (problem -> action -> result, 60-90 s), gaps with
   honest answers, salary (band researched; leveling arguments; never anchored on the current salary),
   questions to ask, logistics and conflicts with other live processes.
4. Iterate on the user's annotations. Every prep carries the "retired claims" fallback: if an older sent
   resume contained a since-retired claim, the prep says how to answer it truthfully.
5. After the interview: debrief notes into the application folder; POST-HM outcomes logged as their own
   Reject Type (learning.md).
