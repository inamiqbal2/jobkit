## What the work actually was
Staff RN on a 24-bed adult medical ICU (level I trauma center, ~500 beds). Primary nursing for one or two critically ill
patients per shift: sepsis, ARDS, DKA, GI bleeds, overdoses, post-cardiac-arrest care. Night shift, three 12-hour shifts
a week plus one extra most months.

## Technical specifics
- Ventilated patients every shift (volume and pressure modes; weaning and spontaneous-breathing trials with RT).
- Vasoactive and sedation drips (norepinephrine, vasopressin, phenylephrine, propofol, dexmedetomidine, fentanyl) titrated
  to unit protocols.
- CRRT (Prismaflex): set up, ran and troubleshot circuits as the bedside nurse (unit-trained, competency signed 2024).
- Arterial lines and central lines: zeroing, waveform interpretation, dressing changes; targeted temperature management
  (Arctic Sun) after cardiac arrest.
- Epic charting (flowsheets, MAR, I&O, Rothman); unit sepsis bundle; CAUTI / CLABSI prevention bundles.
