# Blind profile - Marina Example (FABRICATED, 2026-10-09). What the two blind rubric scorers read for every JD.
## HAS
M.S. Marine Biology (2023) + B.S. Biology. Three years of coral-reef field and lab work: benthic photo-transect
monitoring (14 fixed sites), coral nursery husbandry and outplanting (Oahu and the Florida Keys), structure-from-motion
photogrammetry, PAM fluorometry, water-quality sampling (sonde, nutrients, alkalinity titration), larval rearing and
heat-stress experiments, DNA extraction and PCR. AAUS scientific diver (300+ dives), Rescue Diver, DAN O2, First Aid/CPR;
small-boat crew/operator (supervised). R (tidyverse, lme4, vegan, ggplot2), QGIS/ArcGIS Pro, CoralNet, Metashape,
ImageJ. 2 peer-reviewed publications (1 first author), 2 presentations, 2 semesters as a TA, trained 15 technicians and
volunteers. U.S. citizen, based on Oahu, relocation yes.
## DOES NOT HAVE
A PhD (not eligible for postdocs or "PhD required" roles). A USCG captain's license. Divemaster/instructor rating. Python.
ROV piloting, flow cytometry, multibeam sonar. Grant PI experience. Remote-sensing / satellite specialism.
## TARGET
(1) Paid coral-reef research positions: research assistant / technician, lab manager, field scientist, program
coordinator at universities, marine labs, NOAA partners, state agencies and NGOs. (2) Funded PhD positions, studentships
and graduate research assistantships in coral-reef ecology / restoration / symbiosis (US). (3) Adjacent field-biology
roles (fisheries, seagrass, estuarine, water quality) are welcome and are NOT marked down on "career" for being outside
coral reefs. Fixed-term and grant-funded posts are fine.
## LIMITS / NOT SEEKING
Postdocs; aquarium retail or husbandry-for-display; dive guiding/instruction; cruise/yacht industry; software roles;
clinical/biomedical lab roles; pure desk GIS/remote-sensing roles without field work.
## LEVEL
Early-career scientist (3 years post-B.S., M.S. 2023). "Senior" titles that need 5+ years are a stretch; "I/II",
"assistant", "technician", "coordinator", "graduate assistant" are the right level.
