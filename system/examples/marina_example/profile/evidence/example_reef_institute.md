## What the work actually is
Field and lab technician for a coral-reef monitoring and restoration program on Oahu (NOAA Coral Reef Conservation
Program partner). Lead diver for the benthic monitoring team; runs the coral nursery husbandry schedule; manages the
program's survey and water-quality datasets in R.

## Technical specifics
- Benthic monitoring: 25 m photo-transects at 14 fixed sites, 4 surveys/yr; photoquadrat annotation in CoralNet;
  structure-from-motion photogrammetry (GoPro rigs, Agisoft Metashape) for 6 of the 14 sites since 2024.
- Restoration: Montipora capitata and Porites compressa fragments in an in-water nursery (~1,200 fragments);
  outplanting campaigns Mar/Sep; outplant survival tracked at 1, 6, 12 months.
- Water quality: monthly YSI EXO2 sonde profiles (temperature, salinity, DO, turbidity), nutrient samples (filtered,
  frozen, sent to the UH lab), total-alkalinity titrations in the institute lab (operated).
- Lab: coral tissue sampling for symbiont ID, DNA extractions (Qiagen), PCR; qPCR and sequencing are run by the UH
  genetics core (USED, not operated).
- Data: R (tidyverse, vegan, lme4) scripts for cover and survival analyses; QGIS site maps; data QA/QC and the
  program's metadata records.

## Instruments / tools OPERATED vs USED
