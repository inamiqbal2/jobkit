## What the work actually is
M.S. in Marine Biology. Thesis: thermal tolerance of Montipora capitata recruits under repeated bleaching (advisor:
Dr. A. Example). Graduate research assistant in the coral physiology lab; teaching assistant for two semesters of
Marine Ecology Lab.

## Technical specifics
- Reared M. capitata larvae from the 2022 summer spawning; settled recruits on conditioned tiles; ran a 3-cycle
  repeated-heat-stress experiment (28, 30, 32 C) in a 24-tank flow-through system.
- Measured photosynthetic efficiency (Fv/Fm) with a Diving-PAM; symbiont density and chlorophyll a by spectrophotometry;
  survival and growth by weekly photographs analysed in ImageJ.
- Analysed with mixed-effects models in R (lme4); produced all thesis figures in ggplot2.
- Sampled symbiont communities (ITS2) - extraction and PCR by me, sequencing at the university core (USED).
