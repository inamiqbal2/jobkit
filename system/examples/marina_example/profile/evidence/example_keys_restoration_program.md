# Evidence: Example Keys Restoration Program - Field Technician (seasonal, May 2020 - Jul 2021)
Status: CONFIRMED-BY-USER 2026-10-09 (FABRICATED example)
NDA / disclosure: no nursery coordinates.

## What the work actually is
Seasonal field technician for a coral nursery and outplanting program in the Florida Keys (two seasons, 14 months
total). Nursery maintenance diving, fragment tagging, outplanting and survival surveys; small-boat crew.

## Technical specifics
- Nursery: Acropora cervicornis and A. palmata tree nurseries; cleaned, re-hung and tagged fragments (~3,000 fragments
  handled per season); recorded genotype and condition in the program database.
- Outplanting: cement-and-epoxy outplanting on 9 reef sites; 1- and 6-month survival surveys (bar-tag re-sight).
- Water quality: weekly temperature-logger downloads (HOBO), nursery turbidity notes.
- Boat crew: line handling, anchoring, dive tending on a 26 ft dive boat (not the captain).

## Instruments OPERATED vs USED
OPERATED: HOBO loggers, underwater slates/cameras, epoxy outplanting tools.
USED: program database (data entry only).

## Real numbers with provenance
- ~3,000 fragments handled per season; 9 outplant sites (program season reports 2020, 2021, FABRICATED).
- ~120 dives over the two seasons (dive log).

## Scope
Crew of 4 per boat day; reported to the nursery manager; 14 months total.
