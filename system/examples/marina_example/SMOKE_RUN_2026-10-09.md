## Calibration: before -> after (same 1,567 cached JDs, offline rescoring)

Three defects found by labelling, all fixed in the engine or the workspace:
1. **Empty-corpus TF-IDF** (engine, match.score_texts): the academic pack has no Greenhouse/Lever/Ashby boards, so the corpus is
   empty and the vectorizer was fitted on the profile alone - 700 FIU postings scored m=0.55 flat with no signal. Now, below 200
   corpus docs, it is fitted on profile + the screened JDs.
2. **'Post Doctoral' with a space** escaped the postdoc title drop (eligibility.py) - fixed (`post[- ]?doc`).
3. **Signal terms** firing on plant nurseries and HR text (`nursery`, `recruitment`, `settlement`) tightened to coral contexts;
   negatives added for forestry/botany/vegetation and student-assistant postings; transferable terms widened (field research/site,
   wetland/marsh). Nature/Science RSS entries got a `loc_regex` so non-US items are dropped by geography.
